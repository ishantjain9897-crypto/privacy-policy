# Privacy Policy for Aroha

**Last Updated: October 31, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Aroha** ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). By using Aroha, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect
* **Personal Data:** We may collect your name and email address if you choose to create an account for syncing your syllabus progress.
* **Usage Data:** We collect anonymous data on how you interact with the app (e.g., which chapters you track) to improve the user experience.
* **Device Information:** We may collect your device type and operating system version for troubleshooting and analytics.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
* To provide and maintain the App's features (such as tracking your CBSE Class 11 syllabus progress).
* To notify you about updates or changes to the App.
* To provide customer support and respond to your inquiries.

## 3. Data Sharing and Security
We do not sell your personal data. We use industry-standard security measures (including Firebase services) to protect your information. We do not share your data with third parties except as required by law.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Aroha is designed for students. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

## 5. Your Rights
You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time. You can do this within the App's settings or by contacting us.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
